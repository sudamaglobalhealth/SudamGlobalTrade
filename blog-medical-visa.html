Comprehensive Indian Medical Visa (e-MV) & e-FRRO Regulatory Handbook

Complete 2026 legal guidelines, pre-departure checklists, hospital workflows, and in-country extension protocols for international patients traveling to India.

Visa & Travel Guide Updated: 2026 Edition 12 min read Sudama Global Health

India has emerged as one of the world's primary destinations for complex medical care, offering cutting-edge surgical infrastructure, internationally trained medical specialists, and highly affordable care pathways. To streamline entry for international patients, the Government of India operates the e-Medical Visa (e-MV) ecosystem alongside the e-FRRO (e-Foreigners Regional Registration Office) platform for in-country residency management.

This comprehensive handbook serves as a definitive operational and legal guide for international patients, medical attendant caregivers, referring physicians, and healthcare concierges. Managed in collaboration with Sudama Global Health, this document outlines every critical stage of the international patient journey—from preliminary clinical evaluation to post-operative clearance and visa extension workflows.

📌 Key Resource: This guide covers everything from visa application to hospital admission, extension protocols, and emergency contingencies for international patients visiting India.

1. Visa Categories & Regulatory Specifications

The Indian visa architecture categorizes medical travelers under specific electronic channels depending on their primary objective and their relationship to the primary care recipient:

Visa Category Detailed Regulatory Specifications (2026 Framework)
e-Medical Visa (e-MV) Granted exclusively to foreign nationals traveling to India for specialized medical treatment in accredited hospitals. Valid for 60 days from the date of first entry into India with Triple-Entry privileges. Maximum of N = 3 grants permitted per calendar year per applicant.
e-Medical Attendant Visa (e-MED-X) Granted to accompanying caregivers or family members. A maximum of 2 attendants are allowed per primary patient. The duration and validity run strictly co-terminus with the primary patient's e-Medical Visa.
Standard & Emergency Processing Standard processing requires 42 to 72 hours. Fast-track emergency medical clearances can be issued within 24 to 36 hours upon submission of urgent medical necessity documentation from host hospitals.
Approved Medical Scope Covers specialized procedures including Neurosurgery, Cardiac Surgery, Organ Transplants, Joint Replacements, Oncology, Gene Therapy, and AYUSH treatments. Note: Commercial surrogacy is strictly prohibited under e-MV guidelines.

✈️ Key Compliance Note: Patients must ensure their destination airport or seaport is an officially authorized Immigration Check Post (ICP). India permits entry under e-Medical Visas through 31 designated international airports and 5 major seaports.

2. Essential Application Document Checklist

To avoid processing delays or visa rejection, all supporting documentation uploaded to the official portal must meet strict formatting, clarity, and legal verification standards. The file size for each document upload must not exceed 3 MB.

Document Name Specific Content & Regulatory Requirements Mandatory File Format
Passport Bio Page High-resolution color scan of the biological page. Passport must have minimum validity of V ≥ 6 months from arrival date and at least 2 blank immigration pages. PDF or Clear JPEG
Digital Photograph Recent 2x2 inch square photograph with plain white background, full front-face view, no borders, no dark glasses or unverified headgear. JPEG Format Only
Visa Invitation Letter (VIL) Official hospital letterhead from a recognized Indian hospital detailing patient passport number, medical diagnosis, physician name, treatment plan, and estimated stay. Official PDF Copy
Local Medical Referral Diagnostic reports, clinical summary, or formal referral letter from a certified medical practitioner in the patient's home country verifying treatment necessity. PDF Document
Proof of Financial Capacity Bank statements covering the preceding 3 months or certified financial sponsorship guarantee proving adequate funds for medical fees and living expenses. Official Bank PDF
Attendant Relationship Proof For e-MED-X applicants: Valid legal proof such as a marriage certificate, birth certificate, or sworn family relationship affidavit linking the caregiver to the patient. Legal Document PDF

Detailed Technical Specifications for Document Scanning

  • Resolution & Clarity: Scans must be performed at 300 DPI to ensure all text, official seals, and signatures are legible. Low-resolution or blurred smartphone photos are subject to automated rejection.
  • Name Consistency: The applicant's name on the Visa Invitation Letter (VIL), local medical reports, and passport must match character-for-character. Any discrepancies require an official affidavit of name confirmation.
  • Language Translation: Documents issued in non-English languages must be accompanied by an official, notarized English translation copy.

3. Clinical Protocols & Hospital Admission Procedures

Navigating clinical treatment in India requires structured pre-arrival planning to ensure uninterrupted transition from travel to hospital admission. Sudama Global Health coordinates with accredited medical institutions across major healthcare hubs including New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, and Hyderabad.

Pre-Arrival Clinical Assessment & Virtual Tumor Boards

Prior to international travel, patient diagnostic records—including MRI scans, PET-CT reports, biopsy results, and blood panels—are evaluated by multi-disciplinary specialist panels in India. This virtual pre-assessment enables hospitals to outline a preliminary treatment protocol, estimate length of ICU/ward stay, and issue an accurate Visa Invitation Letter (VIL).

Specialized Medical Specialty Common Procedures Covered Under e-MV Avg. Hospital Stay
Oncology & Hematology Surgical Resection, Chemotherapy, Immunotherapy, CyberKnife, Bone Marrow Transplant (BMT). 14 to 45 Days
Cardiology & Cardiac Surgery CABG (Bypass), Valve Replacement, Pediatric Cardiac Surgery, Transcatheter Aortic Valve Implantation (TAVI). 7 to 14 Days
Organ Transplants Liver Transplant, Kidney Transplant, Heart & Lung Transplant (Requires State Authorization Committee approval). 30 to 90 Days
Orthopedics & Spine Robotic Total Joint Replacement, Complex Spine Fusion, Scoliosis Correction. 7 to 21 Days

🏥 Special Regulatory Framework for Organ Transplants: Foreign nationals undergoing living donor organ transplants in India must comply with the Transplantation of Human Organs Act (THOA). The donor must be a verified close relative, and all documentation must be reviewed and approved by the State-Level Authorization Committee prior to surgery.

4. In-Country Visa Extensions & e-FRRO Compliance

Foreign patients requiring prolonged clinical management beyond their initial 60-day visa validity must fulfill specific legal requirements under the Ministry of Home Affairs guidelines. All registration and extension procedures are processed digitally via the official e-FRRO portal.

Mandatory e-FRRO Regulatory Protocols

  • Medical Visa Extensions: If medical treatment extends beyond the initial 60 days, applications for extension must be submitted online via e-FRRO 2 to 3 weeks prior to visa expiration. Extensions can be granted for up to 1 year (in 6-month increments) based on updated hospital medical certificates.
  • 14-Day Mandatory Registration Rule: Foreign nationals issued long-term medical visas valid for more than 180 days are legally required to register on the e-FRRO platform within 14 days of arrival in India.
  • Form C Lodging Compliance: Hospitals, hotels, guest houses, or private rental accommodations housing foreign patients must submit Form C online within 24 hours of arrival to verify physical residence.
  • Exit Permits: In cases where a patient's visa expires during emergency ICU hospitalization, an official Exit Permit must be secured through e-FRRO prior to departure.

6. Emergency Contingencies & Financial Guidance

International medical travel requires advance financial and logistical planning for unforeseen clinical emergencies or extended intensive care unit (ICU) stays.

  • Financial Remittance & Forex Clearance: Medical payments can be transferred directly to accredited hospital bank accounts via international wire transfer (SWIFT), credit cards, or authorized foreign exchange desks. Official receipts are generated for e-FRRO financial verification.
  • Medevac & Air Ambulance Support: For critically ill patients requiring specialized air transport, Sudama Global Health coordinates tarmac-to-tarmac ICU air ambulance transfers equipped with emergency medical retrieval teams.
  • Post-Discharge Fit-to-Fly Certification: Prior to booking return flights, patients must obtain a formal Fit-to-Fly Certificate from their treating consultant to present to commercial airlines.
